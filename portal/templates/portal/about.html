About the CCS Student Portal

Portal Purpose

The CCS Student Portal is designed to streamline communication between the academic department and its students by providing a single, organized web platform for announcements, schedules, and downloadable academic materials.

Intended Users

This portal is built primarily for undergraduate students enrolled in the department, as well as faculty advisers and administrative staff sharing course updates.

Accessibility Features Implemented

  • High-Contrast Color Palette: Deep navy headers (#16324F) paired with crisp white text and dark slate body copy (#1E293B) on an off-white background (#F8FAFC) to exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Keyboard-Friendly Navigation: Visible high-contrast focus rings (#FBBF24) on all interactive links, semantic <nav> landmarks, and a keyboard-accessible "Skip to main content" link.
  • Descriptive Alternative Text: Meaningful alt attributes on brand imagery so screen readers convey visual context accurately.
